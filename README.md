# -a-Va
At the GWC summer program, I built a small social network with three other classmates. It was called Ça Va. Ça Va was a self-help social network for people with mental illnesses or disabilities, who need an outlet to vent. There were communities for like-minded people to join, including but not limited to: ADHD, Bipolar Disorder, Depression, LGBT+, and Anxiety.
