# Ça Va
At the GWC summer program, I built a small social network with three other classmates. It was called Ça Va. Ça Va was a self-help social network for people with mental illnesses or disabilities, who need an outlet to vent. There were communities for like-minded people to join, some of the several groups created, made safe communities for people including but not limited to: ADHD, Bipolar Disorder, Depression, LGBT+, and Anxiety. 

Since its launch in August of 2016, site access (cava.x10host.com) has been revoked by the domain hoster, preventing us to access the social network. 

Screenshots of the website in use can be found at cava.gq
